# Enemites Research Lab — Abstract inquiry ornament

## Art direction

A non-photographic company identity ornament: two asymmetrical fields of charcoal lines converge around an open center, interrupted by a terracotta accent. The form evokes inquiry, convergence and possibility without depicting a physical product, facility, construction, or event.

Flat graphic character, native transparency, and the website's charcoal/terracotta palette. The graphic stands directly on the ivory page without a photo frame, backing plate, borders, shadows, faded masks, or orbit overlays.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: abstract, non-photographic brand ornament for the Enemites Research Lab homepage. A purely graphic symbol of inquiry, convergence, and the search for ground truth; not a depiction of any physical object, product, workplace, building, or activity.

Create one sophisticated flat 2D abstract graphic on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background, landscape 3:2 canvas. Think of a striking contemporary mathematical art print or experimental editorial identity. The image itself contains no page, border, frame, mount, poster rectangle, or background.

Composition: an original asymmetric convergence form made of two broad fields of clean, closely spaced charcoal ink curves. On the left, a flowing fan of parallel lines opens out widely, then bends and compresses toward a narrow central off-axis seam. On the right, a second more angular field folds outward in a different direction, creating an elegant tension and a sharply defined irregular negative-space aperture between them. A single restrained flat terracotta ribbon interrupts the charcoal line field at the convergence; a small solid terracotta wedge provides one intentional focal accent. Fine lines vary in density to create optical rhythm, but the entire image remains unambiguously FLAT GRAPHIC ART, not a rendered sculpture. Mix fine precise contours with a few broad solid black graphic planes so the silhouette remains strong and legible at mobile sizes. Use expressive controlled asymmetry, broad gestures, deliberate negative space, impeccable alignment and a clean sculptural silhouette defined only by 2D ink. Keep every part inside the canvas with 8 percent clear margin. The form should occupy about 78 percent of the canvas and feel balanced as a large free-standing graphic ornament beside a headline.

Palette: only warm charcoal #272c27, muted terracotta #a64b31, and occasional pale terracotta flat tints. All empty areas truly transparent so the website's ivory paper background shows through naturally.
Style: precise vector-like ink drawing, refined modernist editorial graphic design, abstract and symbolic. Crisp antialiased edges. No text, letters, numbers, logo wordmark, captions, arrows, diagram labels, axes, or charts.
Strictly avoid: photography, photographic lighting, realistic materials, 3D rendering, perspective, isometric drawing, glass, stone, metal, surfaces, realistic textures, cast shadows, drop shadows, ambient occlusion, reflective highlights, a plinth, a pedestal, a lab bench, machinery, construction, architecture, scaffolding, buildings, literal neural networks, node-and-edge diagrams, chips, brains, globes, portals, interlocking arches, ring sculptures, or orbit ornaments. No blur, haze, faded edges, gradients, mockup, border, or rectangular image background. One finished abstract transparent graphic only.
