# B-roll catalog: Realms Competitive launch film

Archival, public-domain footage for the film's "Every great plan needed a team" spine. Every clip is a single clean shot
(unless noted), H.264, no audio, native resolution with letterbox/pillarbox removed and interlaced sources deinterlaced
(IVTC for the NTSC telecine transfers). Media lives in `.work/broll/` (gitignored) and is rebuilt with `broll/fetch.sh`;
full rights evidence, source URLs and exact in/out points are in `broll/credits.json`. Contact sheets: `.work/broll/sheets/sheet-01..05.jpg`.

Grading notes: several sources are colour (Apollo 8 MOCR, SAGE, SAC, ARADCOM, Hoover, Last Bomb); all hold up in an amber/charcoal
duotone. Resolution varies: Apollo 8 MOCR (1440x1064), The River (1488x1072), Chess Fever (1376x1056) and the 1904 Boat Race (1440x1080) are HD film scans; the rest are
SD (626-960 px wide) and will need upscaling plus grain to sit at 1080p, so frame them smaller (inside graphic windows/maps) or behind
heavier texture. Frame rates are native (23.976 / 24 / 29.97; the Eyes Aloft upload is flagged 29 fps): conform to 30 fps in the comp
(frame-blend or hold) rather than relying on the container rate. Times in "best moments" are seconds from the start of each clip.

## Rejected / not used

- PeriscopeFilm uploads (burnt-in timecode/watermarks, claimed scans), British Pathe "Royal Observer Corps" (Pathe claims copyright despite a PD mark), Riefenstahl/1936 Olympics footage (unclear rights, wrong connotations).
- USS Denver Panama Canal transit (NPC-733/734): burnt-in timecode window across the frame.
- Army Olympic Trials 1920 reel: heavy splices and handwritten film-lab numbers on many shots; only the clean tug-of-war shots kept.
- Chess-themed Commons clips from 2025-26 (CC0/PD) look modern and low-res; Hoover Dam "engineers with plans" shot has a dissolve mid-shot.

## Rights notes

- Everything kept is public domain in the US: US federal government works (NASA, Army, Army Air Forces, Navy, Air Force, Bureau of Reclamation, Farm Security Administration; 17 U.S.C. 105) or films published before 1931. No CC-BY, NC or ND material is used, so no attribution is legally required; crediting "Archival footage: NASA, U.S. National Archives, Library of Congress, Prelinger Archives" in the end card is good practice.
- Chess Fever (1925): the film is PD in the US, but the archive.org file is a re-encode of a commercial Blu-ray restoration (a faithful restoration adds no new US copyright; its modern score is discarded). If the team prefers to avoid that file, the same shots exist in the lower-res archive.org item `silent-shakhmatnaya-goryachka-aka-chess-fever` and on Wikimedia Commons (`Chess Fever (Mezhrabpom-Russ, 1925).webm`, 640x480, PD).
- NASA material must not imply NASA endorsement of Enemites; keep NASA insignia out of hero frames (none are prominent in the kept clips).
