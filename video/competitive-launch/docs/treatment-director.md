# Director's treatment (draft A): "The Briefing"

One idea: the whole film is a match briefing that comes alive on ONE continuous topographic map
("One world"). Every scene is a place on that map; cuts are camera moves along routes. Our plan
is drawn in amber, theirs in red-orange. Archival history appears as memories "pinned" to the map,
graded amber, framed by register marks. Restrained, tactical, confident. 120 BPM: 1 bar = 2 s.
